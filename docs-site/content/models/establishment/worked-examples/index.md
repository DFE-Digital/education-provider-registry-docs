# Establishment Ontology — worked examples

Worked examples mapping real-world GIAS establishment records onto `establishment-ontology.ttl` (`est:`/`esto:`), following the same pattern as the [governance worked examples](../../governance/worked-examples/). Each example draws on a real GIAS extract - not synthetic data - and is explicit about where the ontology fits the evidence cleanly and where it doesn't (yet).

| Example | Establishment or trust | Type |
|---|---|---|
| [Co-op Academy Medlock / The Co-operative Academies Trust](./medlock-mat/) | Co-op Academy Medlock (URN 150612) and The Co-operative Academies Trust (GIAS UID 2777, Companies House 07747126) | Academy sponsor led (mainstream academy) — identity, classification, accountability, group membership and sponsorship, location, capacity and pupil measures, SEN and resourced provision |
| [Manor High School / OAK Multi Academy Trust](./manor-high/) | Manor High School (URN 137120) and OAK Multi Academy Trust (GIAS UID 16991, Companies House 07695364) | Academy converter (mainstream academy) — identity, classification, accountability, group membership, location, capacity and pupil measures; a clean single-sponsor contrast to Medlock, with faith context, SEN provision, admissions policy and Section 41 approval all genuinely absent |
| [Frank Barnes School for Deaf Children](./frank-barnes/) | Frank Barnes School for Deaf Children (URN 100091), maintained by Camden | Community special school — first LA-maintained example (no trust, sponsor or group), real SEN need type (hearing impairment), special classes and nursery provision |
| [Eileen Wade / Milton Ernest Federation](./eileen-wade-milton-ernest/) | Eileen Wade Primary School (URN 109443, Foundation school) and Milton Ernest CofE Primary School (URN 109613, Voluntary controlled school), federated under GIAS UID 1809 | First federation example — two different establishment types under one group, shared headteacher across both, contrasting real religious character values (none vs Church of England with diocese) |
| [Long Ditton Federation](./long-ditton/) | Long Ditton Infant and Nursery School (URN 124966, Foundation school) and Long Ditton St Mary's CofE Junior School (URN 125180, Voluntary aided school), federated under GIAS UID 17766 | First real use of `est:GenericTrust` — Long Ditton Infant belongs to both the federation and a separate non-academy trust group simultaneously; a federation split by age range (infant/junior) rather than by faith or governance profile |
| [St Luke's / Moreland Federation](./st-lukes-moreland/) | St Luke's CofE Primary School (URN 100443, Voluntary aided school) and Moreland Primary School (URN 100415, Community school), federated under GIAS UID 1456 | First federation with two different headteachers rather than one shared leader; a voluntary aided school federated with a community school; Moreland's pupil roll exceeds its stated capacity in the real extract |
| [Vauxhall Primary School / Wyvern Federation](./vauxhall-primary/) | Vauxhall Primary School (URN 100588, Foundation school), Wyvil Primary School (URN 100591, Foundation school) and Lilian Baylis Technology School (URN 100625, Community school, secondary), federated under GIAS UID 1951 with three further members | First federation with six member schools rather than two, and the first with a secondary-phase member; real "None" religious character and two simultaneous SEN need types at Wyvil; found and fixed a genuine ontology bug in `classifiedByTypeOfSenProvision`'s cardinality |
| [Gilded Hollins Community School](./gilded-hollins/) | Gilded Hollins Community School (URN 106431), maintained by Wigan | First standalone (non-federation-member) community school - no trust, sponsor or group at all; pupil roll (212) exceeds published capacity (210), the same real over-capacity pattern found at Moreland but on a different governance shape; no SEN or resourced provision at all |
| [Millfield First and Nursery School](./millfield/) | Millfield First and Nursery School (URN 117309), maintained by Hertfordshire | A real "first school" - statutory age range 3 to 9, narrower than every other `est:PrimaryPhase` example so far, confirming education phase and age range are independent facts; second standalone community school, with nursery classes where Gilded Hollins has none |
| [Brookside Primary School / OAK Multi Academy Trust](./oak-brookside/) | Brookside Primary School (URN 145619, Mainstream academy, converter route), member of OAK Multi Academy Trust (GIAS UID 16991, the same Trust as Manor High) | A real combined "resourced provision and SEN unit" value found and fixed as a missing ontology individual; retroactively fixed a "not applicable" placeholder anti-pattern found in Medlock's own instance data; same Trust as Manor High, different phase |
