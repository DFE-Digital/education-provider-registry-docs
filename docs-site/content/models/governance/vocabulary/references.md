# Governance Vocabulary — Real-World References

This page records authoritative or directly relevant sources for the provisional governance vocabulary. It is intentionally evidence-led: a term may be real-world while its exact subdivision or product label remains only partially defined by an authoritative source.

## Authoritative sources used

| Source | Abbreviation | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GOV.UK guidance and policy pages | GOV.UK | Varies by page — see Link quality column. |
| UK legislation ([legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/)) | Legislation | Statutory definitions; most precise for legal form. |
