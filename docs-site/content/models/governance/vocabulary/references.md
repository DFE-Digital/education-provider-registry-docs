# Governance Vocabulary — Real-World References

This page records authoritative or directly relevant sources for the provisional governance vocabulary. It is intentionally evidence-led: a term may be real-world while its exact subdivision or product label remains only partially defined by an authoritative source.

## Authoritative sources used

| Source | Abbreviation | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GOV.UK guidance and policy pages | GOV.UK | Varies by page — see Link quality column. |
| UK legislation ([legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/)) | Legislation | Statutory definitions; most precise for legal form. |
| [Academy Trust Handbook 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/academy-trust-handbook/academy-trust-handbook-2025-effective-from-1-september-2025) | ATH 2025 | DfE's primary non-statutory governance guidance for academy trusts; compliance is a condition of the funding agreement. |
| [Companies Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/contents) | CA 2006 | Academy trusts are companies limited by guarantee; defines member and director. |
| [Charities Act 2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/25/contents) | ChA 2011 | Academy trusts are exempt charities; defines charity trustee. |
| [School Governance (Constitution) (England) Regulations 2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1034/contents) | SI 2012/1034 | Statutory basis for maintained school governing body categories, appointment notification, and disqualification for non-attendance. |
| [Ofsted Education Inspection Framework (November 2025)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/education-inspection-framework/education-inspection-framework-for-use-from-november-2025) | Ofsted EIF | Definitional list of provision types under the EIF. The EIF explicitly names the school and academy types it inspects. |
| [Ofsted inspection report search](https://reports.ofsted.gov.uk/) | Ofsted search | Ofsted's own three-level type taxonomy used in the inspection reports search. |
