# Governance Ontology — worked examples

Worked examples mapping real-world governance evidence onto `governance-ontology.ttl` (`gov:`/`govo:`). Each example draws on a bounded, sourced investigation - not synthetic data - and is explicit about where the ontology fits the evidence cleanly and where it doesn't (yet).

| Example | Trust, academy or school | Type |
|---|---|---|
| [Co-operative Academies Trust / Co-op Academy Medlock](./medlock-mat/) | The Co-operative Academies Trust (GIAS UID 2777, Companies House 07747126) and Co-op Academy Medlock (URN 150612) | MAT — trust board, academy community council, membership, legal capacity, operational and professional support appointments |
| [Frank Barnes School for Deaf Children](./frank-barnes/) | Frank Barnes School for Deaf Children (URN 100091) | Community special school — governing body, statutory governor categories, committees and committee chairing |
| [Manor High School](./manor-high/) | OAK Multi Academy Trust (GIAS UID 16991) and Manor High School (URN 137120) | Academy converter — trust board, local governing body delegation, non-statutory governor categories and governance professional |

**Sources:** "Governance Model With Worked MAT Example: Co-op Academy Medlock", "Frank Barnes School for Deaf Children: governance model worked example" and "Governance Model With Worked Academy Converter Example: Manor High School" - internal DfE transformation investigations drawing on first-party publications and GIAS reconciliation evidence.
