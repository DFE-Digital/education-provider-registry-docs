# Governance Ontology — worked examples

Worked examples mapping real-world governance evidence onto `governance-ontology.ttl` (`gov:`/`govo:`). Each example draws on a bounded, sourced investigation - not synthetic data - and is explicit about where the ontology fits the evidence cleanly and where it doesn't (yet).

| Example | Trust, academy or school | Type |
|---|---|---|
| [Co-operative Academies Trust / Co-op Academy Medlock](./medlock-mat/) | The Co-operative Academies Trust (GIAS UID 2777, Companies House 07747126) and Co-op Academy Medlock (URN 150612) | MAT — trust board, academy community council, membership, legal capacity, operational and professional support appointments |
| [Frank Barnes School for Deaf Children](./frank-barnes/) | Frank Barnes School for Deaf Children (URN 100091) | Community special school — governing body, statutory governor categories, committees and committee chairing |
| [Manor High School](./manor-high/) | OAK Multi Academy Trust (GIAS UID 16991) and Manor High School (URN 137120) | Academy converter — trust board, local governing body delegation, non-statutory governor categories and governance professional |
| [St Paul's Academy](./st-pauls/) | St Benedict Catholic Academy Trust (GIAS group 17729) and St Paul's Academy (URN 105135) | Academy sponsor led — local governing body delegation, foundation/parent/ex-officio governor categories, Chair and a Trust-level governance professional |
| [Eileen Wade / Milton Ernest Federation](./eileen-wade-milton-ernest/) | Federation of Eileen Wade and Milton Ernest CoE Primary Schools (GIAS UID 1809), two maintained schools | Maintained-school federation — one Governing Body shared across two establishments, statutory governor categories, ex-officio headteacher across both schools, and a declaration of interest |
| [Long Ditton Federation](./long-ditton/) | The Long Ditton Federation (GIAS UID 17766), two maintained schools | Maintained-school federation — multiple named foundation appointing bodies, two ex-officio routes to the same role type, Co-Chairs, committees and an Associate Member |
| [Vauxhall Primary School](./vauxhall-primary/) | Vauxhall Primary School (URN 100588) and a shared "Wyvern Federation of Schools" board over six schools | Foundation school, shared board without a GIAS federation record — two Vice-Chairs, six ex-officio headteachers, and committee-only membership without a base board appointment |
| [Gilded Hollins Community School](./gilded-hollins/) | Gilded Hollins Community School (URN 106431), maintained by Wigan Council | Community school — Local Authority accountability, a Chair with no stated category, and a rich set of named subject/portfolio responsibilities across every governor |
| [Millfield First and Nursery School](./millfield/) | Millfield First and Nursery School (URN 117309), maintained by Hertfordshire County Council | Community school — two committees, Committee Chairs and Committee Vice-Chairs, and named link responsibilities |
| [OAK Multi Academy Trust / Brookside Primary School](./oak-brookside/) | OAK Multi Academy Trust (GIAS UID 16991) and Brookside Primary School (URN 145619) | MAT — Trust-level Members, Trustees, Chair/Vice-Chair, specialist Trustee responsibilities and Accounting Officer; shares its Academy Trust and Trust Board instances with the Manor High School worked example |
| [The Green School Trust](./green-school-trust/) | The Green School Trust (GIAS UID 3240, Companies House 08608665), Girls (academy converter) and Boys (free school) academies | MAT — a "Board of Directors" rather than a Trust Board, a corporate Member, one board delegating to two separate Local Governing Bodies, and a person with three distinct relationships |
| [Cheltenham College](./cheltenham-college/) | Cheltenham College (URN 115795), proprietor and governing body "The Council of Cheltenham College" | Other independent school — proprietor and governing body as one instance, charity trustee capacity with no company-director dimension, and a second school sharing the same governing body |
| [The Aldgate School](./aldgate-school/) | The Aldgate School (URN 100000), maintained by the City of London | Voluntary aided school — multiple named foundation appointing bodies, ex-officio foundation governor, and a partnership governor |
| [George Green's School](./george-greens/) | George Green's School (URN 100974), maintained by the London Borough of Tower Hamlets | Voluntary controlled school — a "Governing Board" naming variant, dual and committee-only membership across two committees, and an unresolved committee-chair ambiguity |
