## Definition

A recorded relationship between two establishment groups, for example where a single-academy trust is the predecessor to a newly formed multi-academy trust. Used to preserve conversion history.

## Scope Notes

- Maps to GroupRelationsLink in the current GIAS schema. Currently used primarily to record SAT-to-MAT conversion history.
