## Definition

A general further education college or sixth-form college providing post-16 education and training, operating as an incorporated body under the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.

## Scope Notes

- Covers general FE colleges, tertiary colleges and sixth-form colleges registered in GIAS. Does not include school sixth forms, which are part of their parent school establishment record.
