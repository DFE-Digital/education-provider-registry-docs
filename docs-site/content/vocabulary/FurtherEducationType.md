## Definition

A coded classification that identifies the specific sub-type of provision for establishments in the further education, higher education or pupil referral unit categories. It sub-divides establishment types that are insufficiently distinguished by the main type field alone.

## Scope Notes

- Shown on the Details tab for pupil referral units, further education establishments and higher education institutions.
