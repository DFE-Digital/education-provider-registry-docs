## Definition

The predecessor legal document to the EHC plan, made and maintained by a local authority under s.324 of the Education Act 1996 to record a child's special educational needs. Excluded in England from 1 September 2014 by Schedule 3 to the Children and Families Act 2014; existing statements were transitioned to EHC plans and no new statements can be issued in England.

## Scope Notes

- Historical term, retained in GIAS pupil-count fields alongside EHC plan (SpecialPupilsOnStatementOrEhcPlan, SpecialPupilsNotOnStatementOrEhcPlan) for continuity.
