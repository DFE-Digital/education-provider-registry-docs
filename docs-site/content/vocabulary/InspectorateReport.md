## Definition

A URL or reference to the most recent inspection report published by the named non-Ofsted inspectorate, used for British schools overseas inspected under the British Schools Overseas framework.

## Scope Notes

- Shown for British schools overseas. Different from the Ofsted inspection report link held under External reference.
