## Definition

The accreditation status, dates (start, expiry and end), quality assurance body name and report, Companies House number and accreditation flag for online providers under the DfE online education provider accreditation scheme, introduced in 2021.

## Scope Notes

- Applicable only to online providers (type 49). Accreditation date and accreditation date ended are type-49-specific presentations of the OpenDate and CloseDate fields.
