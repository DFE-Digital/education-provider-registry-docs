## Definition

An education phase for establishments providing early years education to children below compulsory school age, typically ages 3 to 4.

## Scope Notes

- Nursery schools and nursery classes are separately registerable under the Education Act 2002. A standalone nursery school carries this phase; nursery classes within a primary school do not change the school's phase.
