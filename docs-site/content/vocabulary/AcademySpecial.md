## Definition

An academy providing education wholly or mainly for pupils with special educational needs, established under the Academies Act 2010 under a funding agreement with the Secretary of State.

## Scope Notes

- Academy special schools are established via one of two routes, recorded as a separate academy route value: Converter (an existing maintained special school that converted) or Sponsor led (new or takeover provision with an approved sponsor).
