## Definition

The date on which any field in the GIAS establishment record was last updated, or on which the responsible authority last confirmed the record as current and accurate. Used to assess the currency of data held for an establishment.

## Scope Notes

- Describes the currency and confirmation of the GIAS record rather than the lifecycle of the real-world establishment. It may combine two different business events and requires further analysis.
