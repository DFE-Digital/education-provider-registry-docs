## Definition

The establishment type group for all academy establishments: state-funded schools operating outside local-authority maintenance under a funding agreement with the Secretary of State, established under the Academies Act 2010.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 5 types: Mainstream academy, Academy special school, Academy alternative provision, Academy 16 to 19, Academy secure 16 to 19. Mainstream academies, academy special schools, academy alternative provision and academy 16 to 19 establishments each record an academy route value (Converter or Sponsor led) indicating how they were established.
