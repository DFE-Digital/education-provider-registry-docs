## Definition

A special-class-provision value indicating that the establishment provides one or more specially resourced classes or units for pupils with special educational needs.

## Scope Notes

- Special classes or resourced provision within a mainstream school are distinct from a special school. Such provision is relevant under the Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001 and Education Act 1996 Part IV, and may be the basis for a pupil's Education, Health and Care Plan placement.
