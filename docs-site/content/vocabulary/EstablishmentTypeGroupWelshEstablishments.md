## Definition

The establishment type group for schools in Wales that are registered with DfE but governed by Welsh legislation enacted by Senedd Cymru.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 1 type: Welsh establishment. These records appear in GIAS for cross-border data purposes; Welsh school governance is devolved and distinct from the English framework.
