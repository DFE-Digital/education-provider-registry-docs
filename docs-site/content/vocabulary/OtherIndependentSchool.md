## Definition

An independent school registered with the Secretary of State that does not fall into a more specific independent-school category; it charges fees and is not maintained or funded by a local authority or the academy programme.

## Scope Notes

- Distinct from Other independent special school, which is an independent school approved to cater for pupils with special educational needs. City technology colleges are also classified separately within this group.
