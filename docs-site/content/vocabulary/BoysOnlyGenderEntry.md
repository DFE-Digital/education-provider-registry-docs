## Definition

A gender-of-entry value indicating that the establishment admits boys only.

## Scope Notes

- Single-sex admission is permitted for maintained and independent schools under the Equality Act 2010 (s.85 and schedule 11). The recorded value reflects the school's admissions policy, not the composition of its current roll.
