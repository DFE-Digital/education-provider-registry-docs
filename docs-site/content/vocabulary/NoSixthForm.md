## Definition

A sixth-form-provision value indicating that the establishment does not provide a sixth form.

## Scope Notes

- Applies where the sixth-form provision field is applicable to the establishment type (e.g. a secondary school) but no post-16 provision is offered. Distinct from the NotApplicable value, which is used where the field is irrelevant to the establishment type.
