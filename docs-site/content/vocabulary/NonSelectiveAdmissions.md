## Definition

An admissions-policy value indicating that the school does not allocate places through academic selection tests or criteria.

## Scope Notes

- Admissions must comply with the School Admissions Code (statutory under s.84 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998). Non-selective schools may still apply oversubscription criteria such as looked-after children, siblings, or distance.
