## Definition

A religious-character value indicating that the school has a statutory religious character designation for a faith not represented by the other named values in this scheme.

## Scope Notes

- Used where the Secretary of State has conferred a religious character designation under the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 s.69(3) for a religion or denomination other than Church of England, Roman Catholic, Jewish, or Muslim. Examples include Sikh, Hindu, or other Christian denominations.
