## Definition

The name and title of the person with principal leadership responsibility for the establishment. In maintained schools the headteacher is a statutory role under the School Staffing (England) Regulations 2009; for academies the principal role is defined in the funding agreement.

## Scope Notes

- The UI label varies by establishment type. Schools show Headteacher / Principal. Online providers show Headteacher/Manager. Children's centres use the separate CentreManager concept.
