## Definition

A religious-ethos value indicating that the school self-declares a broadly Christian ethos, without specifying a particular denomination.

## Scope Notes

- Religious ethos is a self-declaration by the school or its trust and is not a statutory designation. This value is intentionally broader than Church of England or Roman Catholic character, covering schools that describe a broadly Christian culture. Governed loosely by SSFA 1998 guidance but not a statutory requirement.
