## Definition

The date on which an online provider's accreditation under the DfE scheme was terminated or withdrawn, before the expiry date, resulting in removal from the approved list.

## Scope Notes

- Online-provider-specific presentation of the CloseDate field. The underlying database field is Establishment.CloseDate.
