## Definition

The date on which the group was established. Displayed on group detail pages for federation, school sponsor, children's centre group and collaboration group types.

## Scope Notes

- Not used for trust group types (MAT, SAT, SSAT), which use GroupIncorporatedOnDate instead. The two concepts must not be collapsed.
