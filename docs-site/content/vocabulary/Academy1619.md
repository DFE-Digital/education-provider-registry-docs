## Definition

An academy providing education solely for students aged 16 to 19, established under the Academies Act 2010 under a funding agreement with the Secretary of State.

## Scope Notes

- Academy 16 to 19 establishments are established via one of two routes, recorded as a separate academy route value: Converter (an existing 16-to-19 provider, such as a sixth form college, that converted) or Sponsor led (new provision with an approved sponsor).
