## Definition

An education phase for establishments providing post-16 education and training to young people aged 16 to 19, including sixth-form colleges and studio schools.

## Scope Notes

- Applies to establishments whose primary purpose is post-16 provision. In GIAS this phase is recorded for sixth-form colleges and some free schools. Local authorities have duties to secure sufficient 16-19 provision under the Education Act 2002.
