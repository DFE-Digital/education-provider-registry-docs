## Definition

A classification value indicating that an admissions policy classification does not apply to this type of establishment.

## Scope Notes

- Used for establishment types not subject to school admissions legislation, such as further education colleges, early years settings, or non-maintained special schools. Distinct from non-selective: the field is conceptually inapplicable, not merely answered in the negative.
