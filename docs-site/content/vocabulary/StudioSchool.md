## Definition

A small academy established under the Academies Act 2010 and Education Act 2011 through the free-school programme, typically for students aged 14 to 19, delivering mainstream qualifications through project-based and work-related learning with employer involvement.

## Scope Notes

- The studio schools programme was discontinued by DfE; no new studio schools have been approved since 2017. Existing studio schools continue to operate as academies.
