## Definition

An education phase for establishments providing primary education, broadly covering ages 4 or 5 to 11, within the compulsory school age range defined by the Education Act 1996 s.8.

## Scope Notes

- Includes infant schools (ages 4-7), junior schools (ages 7-11), and combined infant and junior schools. The phase is determined by the Secretary of State and affects Ofsted inspection framework and national curriculum requirements.
