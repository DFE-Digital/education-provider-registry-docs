## Definition

An academy providing alternative provision for pupils who cannot attend mainstream school, established under the Academies Act 2010 under a funding agreement with the Secretary of State.

## Scope Notes

- Academy alternative provision establishments are established via one of two routes, recorded as a separate academy route value: Converter (an existing alternative provision establishment, such as a pupil referral unit, that converted) or Sponsor led (new provision with an approved sponsor).
