## Definition

A classification value indicating that the education phase field is not applicable to this type of establishment.

## Scope Notes

- Used for establishment types where a phase classification is conceptually inapplicable, such as further education colleges, higher education institutions, or local authority children's centres. Distinct from the absence of a value: a phase simply does not apply.
