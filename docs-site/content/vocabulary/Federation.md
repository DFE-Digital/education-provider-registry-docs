## Definition

A formal statutory arrangement in which two or more maintained schools are governed by a single federated governing body. Each member school retains its own URN, establishment number, admissions arrangements and Ofsted inspection identity. Some governance and funding arrangements may be shared or pooled under federation rules. Applicable only to maintained schools, including maintained nursery schools; academy grouping uses the trust model. Source: School Governance (Federations) Regulations 2012.

## Scope Notes

- Federations apply only to maintained schools. An academy cannot join a federation - academies are grouped through the trust model instead. This distinction is confirmed as a business rule.
