## Definition

The establishment type group for providers of full-time online education to pupils of compulsory school age, recognised under the DfE online education accreditation scheme.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 1 type: Online provider. The scheme has no primary statutory basis; providers are registered in GIAS under a DfE regulatory framework only.
