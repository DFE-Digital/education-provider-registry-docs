## Definition

The unique numerical identifier assigned by DfE when an establishment record is created in GIAS, issued under the Education (School Information) Regulations 2008. Every maintained school, academy, independent school and FE college registered with DfE holds a URN, which remains fixed for the lifetime of the record even if the establishment changes name or type.

## Scope Notes

- URNs in the range 100001-199999 are typically assigned to maintained schools; ranges from 130000 are commonly academies; 200000 and above are typically independent schools. The URN is the primary key for GIAS records and should be used in preference to other identifiers when referencing a specific establishment.
