## Definition

The classification of the governance role held. Enumeration of 22 values: 20 from the GIAS governance extract, plus headteacher ex-officio governor and associate member from the maintained schools governance guide (SI 2012/1034, SI 2013/1624).

## Scope Notes

- Three structural groups: maintained-school roles (Governor, Chair of Governors, Headteacher ex-officio, Associate member, Governance Professional LA); academy establishment-level roles (Local Governor, Chair of LGB, Local Governance Professional, Shared establishment variants); trust/MAT roles (Trustee, Member, Chair of Trustees, Accounting Officer, CFO, Governance Professional SAT/MAT, Shared group variants, Governance Professional Federation).
