## Definition

The establishment type group for special schools that are not maintained by a local authority, comprising non-maintained special schools approved under s.342 of the Education Act 1996 and other independent special schools.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 2 types: Non-maintained special school and Other independent special school. Non-maintained special schools differ from other independent special schools in that they are approved by the Secretary of State specifically for SEN pupils and operate on a not-for-profit basis.
