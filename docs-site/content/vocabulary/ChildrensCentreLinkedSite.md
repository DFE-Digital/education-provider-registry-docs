## Definition

A site that delivers some early childhood services on behalf of a named children's centre, having previously been registered as a standalone children's centre under the Childcare Act 2006 framework.

## Scope Notes

- Linked sites are operationally dependent on a parent children's centre and do not hold independent registration. They are recorded in GIAS as a separate establishment type to preserve historical registration data.
