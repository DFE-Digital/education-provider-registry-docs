## Definition

The statutory religious-character designation assigned to an establishment by the Secretary of State under s.69(3) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (for maintained schools) or specified in the funding agreement (for academies). This is a formal legal designation, distinct from the self-declared religious ethos.
