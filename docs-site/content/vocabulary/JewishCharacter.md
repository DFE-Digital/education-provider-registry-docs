## Definition

A religious-character value indicating that the school has been designated by the Secretary of State as having Jewish religious character.

## Scope Notes

- Religious character designation is conferred under s.69(3) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. The designating body for Jewish schools is typically the Board of Deputies of British Jews or another recognised Jewish educational authority.
