## Definition

A boarding-provision value indicating that the establishment accommodates some pupils as boarders, but is not primarily classified as a boarding school.

## Scope Notes

- Applies to schools that provide day education as their primary function but also offer boarding for some pupils. Boarding provision in such schools remains subject to the National Minimum Standards for Boarding Schools and the Education (Independent School Standards) Regulations 2014.
