## Definition

An educational institution whose principal funding comes from a government department other than the Department for Education, and which is recorded in GIAS for cross-government data purposes.

## Scope Notes

- Includes providers such as Ministry of Defence-funded schools or Home Office-related provisions. The specific department and funding basis varies by record.
