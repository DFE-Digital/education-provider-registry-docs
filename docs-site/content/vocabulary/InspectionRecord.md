## Definition

The non-Ofsted inspectorate name, inspection report link and inspection visit dates recorded for an establishment. Applies to independent schools and British schools overseas inspected by bodies such as ISI or the British Schools Overseas inspectorate.

## Scope Notes

- Shown for independent schools (inspectorate name only) and British schools overseas (inspectorate name, report and visit dates).
