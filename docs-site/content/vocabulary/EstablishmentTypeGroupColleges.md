## Definition

The establishment type group for further education colleges and sixth form centres, which provide post-16 and adult education regulated under the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 2 types: Further education and Sixth form centre. Does not include university technical colleges or studio schools, which appear under Free schools.
