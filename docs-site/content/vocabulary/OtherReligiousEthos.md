## Definition

A religious-ethos value indicating that the school self-declares a religious ethos of a faith or denomination not represented by the other named values in this scheme.

## Scope Notes

- Religious ethos is a self-declaration and is not a statutory designation. This value covers schools declaring an ethos such as Jewish, Muslim, Hindu, Sikh, or another faith not captured in the named values.
