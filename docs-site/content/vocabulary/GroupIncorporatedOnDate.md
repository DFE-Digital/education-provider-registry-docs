## Definition

The Companies Act incorporation date for trust group types (MAT, SAT, SSAT). Displayed on group detail pages as 'Incorporated on (open date)'.

## Scope Notes

- Distinct from GroupOpenDate, which is used for non-trust group types. Trust types are incorporated under the Companies Act 2006 and receive a formal incorporation date, which differs semantically from the operational open date of non-trust groups.
