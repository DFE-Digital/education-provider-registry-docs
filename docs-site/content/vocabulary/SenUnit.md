## Definition

A special provision within a mainstream school where pupils with SEN are taught mainly within separate classes for at least half of the time, distinguished from resourced provision by that proportion of time spent outside mainstream classes.

## Scope Notes

- Its numeric measures (capacity and pupil count on roll) are recorded separately as SenUnitCapacity and SenUnitPupilCount. DfE's 2026 SEND reform consultation proposes replacing 'SEN unit', 'resourced provision' and 'pupil support unit' with a single new term 'Inclusion Bases'.
