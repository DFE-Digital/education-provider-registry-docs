## Definition

A school operating in a Crown Dependency, British Overseas Territory or other non-mainland-UK geography that is registered with GIAS under Ministry of Defence or DfE arrangements.

## Scope Notes

- Distinct from British schools overseas (which operate in foreign countries under the BSO inspection framework) and Welsh establishments (which are governed by devolved Welsh legislation). Offshore schools are subject to varying inspection and regulatory regimes depending on jurisdiction.
