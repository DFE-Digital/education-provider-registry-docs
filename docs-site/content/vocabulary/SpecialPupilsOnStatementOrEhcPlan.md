## Definition

The number of pupils with a special educational needs (SEN) statement or Education, Health and Care (EHC) plan under the Children and Families Act 2014 s.37. EHC plans replaced statements from 2014; legacy statements are being phased out.

## Scope Notes

- Shown for independent special schools and online providers in place of the SEN provision type classification used by maintained schools.
