## Definition

Operational indicators and place counts specific to pupil referral units established under s.19 of the Education Act 1996, covering provision for teenage mothers, childcare, SEN, EBD pupils, full-time education and education by other providers.

## Scope Notes

- Shown on the Details tab for pupil referral units (type 14). Not applicable to mainstream or independent schools.
