## Definition

A religious-ethos value indicating that the school does not self-declare any religious ethos.

## Scope Notes

- Religious ethos is a self-declaration and is not a statutory designation. Applies where the ethos field is applicable to the establishment type but the school has declared no religious ethos. Distinct from NotApplicableReligiousEthos, which indicates the field is irrelevant to the establishment type.
