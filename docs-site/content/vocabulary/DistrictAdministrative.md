## Definition

The administrative district in which the establishment is located. Displayed on the establishment location tab in GIAS.

## Scope Notes

- In two-tier areas (county plus district) this is a distinct council below the county. In unitary authority areas, metropolitan districts and London boroughs the district and local authority are the same body, so this will match the LocalAuthority value. Included for consistency with GIAS UI display.
