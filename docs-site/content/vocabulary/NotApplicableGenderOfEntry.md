## Definition

A classification value indicating that a gender-of-entry classification does not apply to this type of establishment.

## Scope Notes

- Used for establishment types where a gender-of-entry classification is conceptually inapplicable, such as further education colleges or early years settings. Distinct from mixed gender entry: the field is not applicable, not merely answered as mixed.
