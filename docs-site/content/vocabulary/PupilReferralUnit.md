## Definition

A local-authority establishment maintained under s.19 of the Education Act 1996 to provide education for children of compulsory school age who are excluded, persistently absent, ill or otherwise unable to attend a mainstream school.

## Scope Notes

- PRUs can convert to alternative provision academies or free schools; those that have done so will appear under the Academies or Free schools type groups instead.
