## Definition

The date on which the establishment ceased operation and its status was recorded as Closed in GIAS. This marks the end of the establishment's active registration.

## Scope Notes

- For online providers (type 49) this field is relabelled Accreditation date ended on the Details tab. See AccreditationDateEnded.
