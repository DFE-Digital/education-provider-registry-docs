## Definition

A dated record of an establishment's membership of an establishment group, trust, federation, sponsor arrangement or children's centre collaboration. An establishment may have membership in more than one group simultaneously.

## Scope Notes

- Maps to GroupLink in the current GIAS schema. Contains the establishment (via URN), the group (via group ID), and an effective date.
