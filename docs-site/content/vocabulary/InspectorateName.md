## Definition

The name of the inspectorate responsible for inspecting the establishment, such as ISI (Independent Schools Inspectorate) for independent schools or the relevant body for British schools overseas. Ofsted is not recorded here; its data appears under External reference.

## Scope Notes

- Shown for independent schools (database field: Inspectorate) and British schools overseas (database field: InspectorateName). Both appear under the same label on the Details tab.
