## Definition

A coded classification that identifies the delivery model or operator category for a children's centre or linked site. It serves the same sub-classification role for children's centres that FurtherEducationType serves for FE/HEI/PRU establishments.

## Scope Notes

- Shown on the Details tab for children's centres and children's centre linked sites in place of the school-type label used by other establishment types.
