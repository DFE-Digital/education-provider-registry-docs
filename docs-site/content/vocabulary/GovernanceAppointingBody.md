## Definition

The body or mechanism responsible for appointing the governor, trustee or member. Enumeration of 18 modelled values, including governing board, foundation, parents, school staff, academy members, local authority, trustees and ex-officio mechanisms.

## Scope Notes

- Source: GIAS governance extract field Appointing body (primary); maintained schools governance guide; SI 2012/1034; academy trust governance guide; model articles of association. One legacy value (Regional Schools Commissioner) retired. Not typically populated for Accounting Officer and Chief Financial Officer roles.
