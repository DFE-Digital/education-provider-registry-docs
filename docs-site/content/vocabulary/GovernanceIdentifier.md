## Definition

The GIAS-assigned identifier for a governance appointment record. Unique per governance row. Not a person identifier - one physical person may have multiple GIDs across different roles and establishments.

## Scope Notes

- GID identifies the appointment record, not the individual. Matching individuals across GIDs is not supported by this identifier alone. Source: GIAS governance extract.
