## Definition

A registered education provider recorded in GIAS, including schools (maintained, academy and independent), further education colleges, children's centres, pupil referral units and online providers. Each establishment is assigned a unique reference number (URN) by the Department for Education.

## Scope Notes

- Do not use school as a universal synonym because GIAS includes non-school establishments.
