## Definition

The registration number assigned by Companies House, the UK registrar of companies under the Companies Act 2006, for the legal entity operating the online provider.

## Scope Notes

- Currently recorded in GIAS for online providers (via AccreditationRecord) and for academy trusts and other group organisations (via group identity). The same concept applies in both contexts.
