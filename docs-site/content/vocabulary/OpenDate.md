## Definition

The date on which the establishment first opened or was first registered with DfE in its current form. For academies this is typically the date the funding agreement took effect; for maintained schools it is the date the school first admitted pupils under its current registration.

## Scope Notes

- For online providers (type 49) this field is relabelled Accreditation date on the Details tab. See AccreditationDate.
