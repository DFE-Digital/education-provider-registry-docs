## Definition

The persistent numeric identifier assigned to a physical site by Ordnance Survey under the Public Sector Mapping Agreement, enabling cross-service geographic linking.

## Scope Notes

- UPRN identifies a physical property (a site), not a postal address. Updated from broader epr:EstablishmentLocationAndContact to epr:Site in v1.5 to reflect that UPRN belongs on the physical location record.
