## Definition

An education phase for establishments providing secondary education, broadly covering ages 11 to 16, and up to 18 where sixth-form provision is included, within the compulsory school age range defined by the Education Act 1996 s.8.

## Scope Notes

- Includes secondary modern, grammar, and comprehensive schools. The phase affects Ofsted inspection framework, national curriculum key stages 3 and 4, and statutory assessment requirements.
