## Definition

A religious-character value indicating that the school has been designated by the Secretary of State as having Muslim religious character.

## Scope Notes

- Religious character designation is conferred under s.69(3) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. Most Muslim-ethos schools are independent or free schools; relatively few hold a formal statutory designation.
