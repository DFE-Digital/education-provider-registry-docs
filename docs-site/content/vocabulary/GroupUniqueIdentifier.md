## Definition

The internal GIAS numeric group identifier, analogous to URN for establishments. Primary identifier for a group within GIAS. Displayed on group detail pages.

## Scope Notes

- Distinct from GroupId, which is the business-facing identifier carrying a TR or SP prefix. The UID is the GIAS surrogate key for the group record.
