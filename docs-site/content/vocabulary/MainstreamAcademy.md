## Definition

A mainstream academy operating under the Academies Act 2010, providing general education outside local-authority maintenance under a funding agreement with the Secretary of State.

## Scope Notes

- Mainstream academies are established via one of two routes, recorded as a separate academy route value: Converter (an existing maintained school that voluntarily converted) or Sponsor led (a school taken on by or established with an approved sponsor).
