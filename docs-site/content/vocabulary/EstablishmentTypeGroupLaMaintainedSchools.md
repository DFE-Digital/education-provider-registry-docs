## Definition

The establishment type group for schools maintained by a local authority under Part II of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998, funded through the dedicated schools grant and subject to LA oversight.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 8 types: Community school, Community special school, Foundation school, Foundation special school, Local authority nursery school, Pupil referral unit, Voluntary aided school, Voluntary controlled school.
