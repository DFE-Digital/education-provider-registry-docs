## Definition

A legal document setting out a child or young person's special educational needs and the special educational, health and social care provision required to meet them, prepared and maintained by the local authority under s.37 of the Children and Families Act 2014.

## Scope Notes

- Referenced in GIAS by the pupil counts recorded against SpecialPupilsOnStatementOrEhcPlan and SpecialPupilsNotOnStatementOrEhcPlan; not itself a displayed count field.
