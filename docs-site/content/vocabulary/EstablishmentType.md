## Definition

The legal, operational or policy classification assigned to an establishment, distinguishing its governance model, funding route and statutory basis within the English education system.

## Scope Notes

- The label 'school type' is too narrow for the wider registry, which includes colleges, universities, children's centres and online providers. The classification conflates legal form, sector and operational model within a single dimension; each leaf concept is grouped under one of 11 type groups.
