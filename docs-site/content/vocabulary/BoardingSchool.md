## Definition

A boarding-provision value indicating that the establishment is registered and operates principally as a boarding school, where pupils reside during term time.

## Scope Notes

- Boarding schools must meet the National Minimum Standards for Boarding Schools and are subject to the Education (Independent School Standards) Regulations 2014. Distinct from HasBoarders: a boarding school's primary identity is as a boarding provision, not merely a day school that also accommodates some boarders.
