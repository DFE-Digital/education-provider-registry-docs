## Definition

The administrative and statistical area classifications assigned to an establishment based on its postcode location. These are derived from the postcode via the GeoData lookup table or an equivalent postcode-to-area service.

## Scope Notes

- Distinct from the postal address (MainAddress). GIAS stores these as separate field groups: ADDRESS for postal address fields and REGION for administrative geography codes. The target model follows the same separation. Administrative geography applies to all establishment types; values are derived from postcode and not independently editable.
