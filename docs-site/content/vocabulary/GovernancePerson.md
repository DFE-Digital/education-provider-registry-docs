## Definition

A named individual who holds or has held a formal governance role at an establishment, establishment group or trust. Covers governors, trustees, members, local governors, accounting officers, chief financial officers and governance professionals. One GovernancePerson may be linked from multiple GovernanceAppointment records.

## Scope Notes

- GovernancePerson covers the full range of governance role types. Personal data - requires access-control treatment. Policy purpose: identify individuals who govern in more than one context (national database of governors).
