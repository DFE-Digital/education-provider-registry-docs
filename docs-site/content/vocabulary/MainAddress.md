## Definition

The primary postal address of the establishment as recorded in GIAS, comprising street, locality, address line 3, town, county and postcode.

## Scope Notes

- Superseded in v1.5 by epr:Address and epr:Site. The concept conflated the postal address and the physical location. Use epr:Address for postal and correspondence addresses; use epr:Site for the physical location where education is delivered.
