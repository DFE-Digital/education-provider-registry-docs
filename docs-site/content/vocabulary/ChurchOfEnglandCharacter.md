## Definition

A religious-character value indicating that the school has been designated by the Secretary of State as having Church of England character, conferring status as a Church of England school.

## Scope Notes

- Religious character designation is conferred under s.69(3) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 Part IV. Church of England schools may be voluntary aided, voluntary controlled, or academy schools with a Church of England academy trust. The designating body is typically the Church of England diocese.
