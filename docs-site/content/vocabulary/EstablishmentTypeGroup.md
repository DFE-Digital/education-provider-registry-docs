## Definition

A coarse classification that groups related establishment types into named sectors or programme families, such as Academies, Local authority maintained schools or Independent schools.

## Scope Notes

- There are 11 type groups: Academies, Children's centres, Colleges, Free schools, Independent schools, Local authority maintained schools, Non-maintained special schools, Online providers, Other types, Universities and higher education, and Welsh establishments.
