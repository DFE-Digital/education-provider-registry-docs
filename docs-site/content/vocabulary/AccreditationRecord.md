## Definition

A grouping of the accreditation fields specific to online providers under the DfE online education provider accreditation scheme: accreditation date, expiry date, date ended, quality assurance body name and report, Companies House number and accreditation status flag. This accreditation is not currently underpinned by primary legislation.
