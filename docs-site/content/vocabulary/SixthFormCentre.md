## Definition

A centre providing sixth-form education for students aged 16 to 18, operating under the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 and typically sharing facilities with or attached to a maintained school.

## Scope Notes

- Sixth form centres are distinct from standalone sixth-form colleges, which appear under the Further education type. They may serve pupils from multiple feeder schools.
