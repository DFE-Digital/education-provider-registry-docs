## Definition

A composite identifier formed by concatenating the three-digit LA number and the establishment number (zero-padded to four digits), conventionally written with a slash separator (e.g. "123/4567"). Used in DfE correspondence, grant letters and official returns to identify a school within its local authority.

## Scope Notes

- The DfE number is derived from its two component fields rather than stored as a single physical column in the Establishment table.
