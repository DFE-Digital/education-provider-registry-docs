## Definition

An independent school that specialises in provision for pupils with special educational needs but is not approved by the Secretary of State under s.342 of the Education Act 1996 as a non-maintained special school.

## Scope Notes

- These schools may be approved by the Secretary of State under s.41 of the Children and Families Act 2014 for the purposes of EHC plan placements, but they are operated on an independent, fee-charging basis.
