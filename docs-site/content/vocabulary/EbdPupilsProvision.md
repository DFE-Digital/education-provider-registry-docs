## Definition

Whether the pupil referral unit admits pupils with Emotional and Behavioural Difficulties (EBD). EBD is a legacy classification in GIAS; it has been superseded by Social, Emotional and Mental Health (SEMH) needs in the SEND Code of Practice 2015.
