## Definition

A classification value indicating that the religious character designation field does not apply to this type of establishment.

## Scope Notes

- Used for establishment types outside the scope of the statutory religious character designation under the School Standards and Framework Act 1998, such as further education colleges. Distinct from NoReligiousCharacter, which indicates the field is applicable but no designation has been conferred.
