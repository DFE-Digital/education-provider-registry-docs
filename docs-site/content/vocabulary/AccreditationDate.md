## Definition

The date on which the online provider was granted accreditation under the DfE online education provider accreditation scheme, enabling it to appear on the DfE approved list.

## Scope Notes

- Online-provider-specific presentation of the OpenDate field. The underlying database field is Establishment.OpenDate.
