## Definition

A school outside the United Kingdom that provides a British-style curriculum and was subject to voluntary inspection by Ofsted or an approved inspectorate under the Education (British Schools Abroad) Order 1999.

## Scope Notes

- Ofsted inspection of British schools overseas ended in 2021 following revocation of the relevant inspection provisions. Records for schools previously holding BSO status may remain in GIAS.
