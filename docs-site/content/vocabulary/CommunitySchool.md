## Definition

A maintained school established under Part II of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 in which the local authority employs all staff, owns the land and buildings, and acts as the admissions authority.

## Scope Notes

- The most common LA-maintained school type. Governing bodies set the ethos but the local authority retains greater control than in foundation or voluntary schools.
