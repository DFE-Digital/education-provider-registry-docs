## Definition

A state-funded independent school in an urban area, established under s.105 of the Education Reform Act 1988 with a curriculum emphasis on technology, science and practical skills, jointly funded by central government and private sponsors.

## Scope Notes

- A small, closed cohort: no new CTCs have been created since the early 1990s. Many have since converted to academies. Classified under Independent schools in GIAS despite being state-funded.
