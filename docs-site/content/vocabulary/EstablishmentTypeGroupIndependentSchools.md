## Definition

The establishment type group for schools that charge fees and are not maintained by a local authority, operating outside the state-funded maintained and academy sectors.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 2 types: City technology college and Other independent school. Note that city technology colleges are state-funded but classified as independent schools in GIAS; independent special schools appear under Non-maintained special schools.
