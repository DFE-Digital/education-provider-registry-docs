## Definition

A grouping of the operational fields specific to pupil referral units established under s.19 of the Education Act 1996, covering: provision for teenage mothers and places, childcare facilities, SEN facilities, EBD pupils, total number of places, full-time provision, and whether pupils are educated by other providers.
