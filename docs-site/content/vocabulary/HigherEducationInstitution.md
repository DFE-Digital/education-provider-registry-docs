## Definition

A university or other higher education provider recognised under the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 and the Higher Education Act 2004, offering degree-level and postgraduate qualifications.

## Scope Notes

- Higher education institutions are registered in GIAS primarily to support recording of university sponsorship relationships with academies and UTCs, rather than for regulatory oversight of the HEI itself.
