## Definition

A body approved by the Secretary of State to sponsor one or more sponsor-led academies or free schools. The sponsor supports governance, leadership and educational improvement of the sponsored academies. Source: Academies Act 2010.

## Scope Notes

- Legacy GIAS group type 05. In the target EPR model the sponsoring body is modelled as an epr:Organisation linked to an epr:Establishment via epro:sponsoredBy. Sponsor is a role an organisation plays, not a type of establishment group.
