## Definition

A nursery-provision value indicating that the establishment operates one or more nursery classes for children below compulsory school age.

## Scope Notes

- Nursery classes within a school are distinct from a separately registered nursery school. The presence of nursery classes does not change the school's phase classification. Provision of nursery education is addressed by local authorities under the Education Act 2002 s.50.
