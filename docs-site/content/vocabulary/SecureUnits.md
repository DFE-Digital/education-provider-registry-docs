## Definition

Secure residential accommodation for children and young people that includes an educational function, recorded in GIAS for cross-departmental data purposes.

## Scope Notes

- Secure units are not mainstream educational establishments; they operate under youth justice or welfare frameworks rather than the standard school regulatory regime. Inclusion in GIAS reflects the need to track education provision for children in secure settings.
