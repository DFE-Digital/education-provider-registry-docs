## Definition

A HESA-assigned identifier used in further- and higher-education data returns, distinct from the UKPRN. Recorded in GIAS for FE colleges and HEIs that submit data to HESA.

## Scope Notes

- The FE/HE identifier is a legacy field and may not be populated for all FE/HE providers in GIAS. Where both this identifier and a UKPRN are present, the UKPRN is the preferred cross-sector reference.
