## Definition

The percentage of pupils on roll who are recorded as eligible for free school meals at the census date, derived from the count of eligible pupils divided by total pupil count.

## Scope Notes

- Receiving FSM is a misleading legacy alias because the measure concerns eligibility claims, not whether pupils received meals.
