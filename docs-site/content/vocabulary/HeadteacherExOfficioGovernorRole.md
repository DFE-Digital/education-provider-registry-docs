## Definition

Headteacher of a maintained school holding a governor role by virtue of their office. Defined in the School Governance (Constitution) (England) Regulations 2012 (SI 2012/1034). Modelled from the maintained schools governance guide; not separately enumerated in the GIAS governance extract.
