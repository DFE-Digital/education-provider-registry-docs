## Definition

A sixth-form-provision value indicating that the establishment provides post-16 education as a sixth form, catering to pupils typically in Years 12 and 13.

## Scope Notes

- Sixth-form provision is a separately recorded field in GIAS. The presence of a sixth form does not change the school's phase classification. Local authorities have duties to secure sufficient 16-19 provision under the Education Act 2002.
