## Definition

The establishment type group for establishment types that do not belong to the mainstream maintained, academy, independent, further education or university sectors.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 7 types: British schools overseas, Institution funded by other government department, Miscellaneous, Offshore schools, Secure units, Service children's education, Special post 16 institution.
