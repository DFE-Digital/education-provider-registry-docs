## Definition

A county council, metropolitan district council, London borough council or unitary authority in England with education functions under the Education Act 1996. In GIAS, the local authority field records the LA in whose area the establishment is located and provides the three-digit LA number used in the DfE number and LAESTAB.

## Scope Notes

- An establishment's local authority is not always its maintaining authority. Academies and independent schools have a local authority for geographic reference purposes but are not maintained by it. See MaintainingAuthority for the specific maintaining role.
