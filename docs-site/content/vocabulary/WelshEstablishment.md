## Definition

A school or other education provider in Wales that is registered with the Department for Education in GIAS but is governed by Welsh legislation enacted by Senedd Cymru, including the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013.

## Scope Notes

- Welsh establishments appear in GIAS for cross-border data-sharing and funding purposes. Their governance, inspection (Estyn, not Ofsted) and regulatory framework differ from English establishments.
