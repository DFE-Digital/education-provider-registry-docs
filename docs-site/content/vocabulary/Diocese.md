## Definition

The ecclesiastical diocese associated with the establishment. For Church of England schools the relevant body is the Diocesan Board of Education; for Roman Catholic schools it is the Catholic Education Service diocese.

## Scope Notes

- Observed on the Details tab for most school types with a religious character. A database-mapping gap exists for non-maintained special schools (type 08); the underlying configuration should be verified.
