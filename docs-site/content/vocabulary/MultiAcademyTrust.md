## Definition

A single legal academy-trust entity incorporated under the Companies Act 2006 that operates two or more academies, free schools, UTCs or studio schools. It has a master funding agreement with the Secretary of State and a supplemental funding agreement for each individual academy. Governed by a board of trustees. Source: GOV.UK academy trust governance guide.

## Scope Notes

- An academy trust operating two or more academies. Distinct from a single-academy trust, which operates exactly one academy. A SAT can convert to a MAT by adding an academy.
