## Definition

A body - typically a school, university, charity or business - approved by the Secretary of State under the Academies Act 2010 to sponsor one or more academies. A sponsor provides strategic leadership and improvement support, particularly for sponsored academies that have converted from underperforming maintained schools.

## Scope Notes

- Shown on the Details tab for sponsor-led academies and several academy and free school types. In the target EPR model the sponsor is an epr:Organisation linked to the establishment via epro:sponsoredBy. The epr:AcademySponsor OWL class has been removed; the concept is retained here as a vocabulary entry for the GIAS UI field.
