## Definition

The name and title of the manager responsible for a children's centre or children's centre linked site, displayed in GIAS under the label 'Manager'.

## Scope Notes

- Children's centres and children's centre linked sites display Manager rather than Headteacher or Principal. The underlying database fields are the same Head* fields used for other establishment types.
