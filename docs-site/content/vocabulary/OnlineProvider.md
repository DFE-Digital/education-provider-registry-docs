## Definition

A provider that delivers full-time education entirely online to pupils of compulsory school age, recognised under the DfE online education accreditation scheme.

## Scope Notes

- The scheme operates under a DfE regulatory framework without a primary statutory basis. Providers must meet DfE quality standards to be listed; registration in GIAS does not confer the same status as a maintained school or academy.
