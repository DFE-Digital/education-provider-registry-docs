## Definition

The date on which the governance appointment ended or is due to end. When unpopulated, absence does not confirm a current appointment - term-end status requires business-rule validation.

## Scope Notes

- Source: GIAS governance extract field Date term of office ends/ended. Absence for Member role is structurally expected - members do not have fixed terms.
