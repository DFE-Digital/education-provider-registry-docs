## Definition

The establishment type group for higher education institutions providing degree-level and postgraduate education, operating under the Higher Education Act 2004 and Further and Higher Education Act 1992.

## Scope Notes

- Contains 1 type: Higher education institution. Universities are registered in GIAS primarily for the purpose of recording university technical college and academy trust sponsorship relationships.
