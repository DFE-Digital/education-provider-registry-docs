## Definition

A 16-to-19 education provider - such as a sixth form college or school sixth form - that converted to academy status under the Academies Act 2010, entering into a funding agreement with the Secretary of State.

## Scope Notes

- Distinct from Academy 16 to 19 sponsor led, which is a newly created 16-to-19 academy rather than a conversion.
