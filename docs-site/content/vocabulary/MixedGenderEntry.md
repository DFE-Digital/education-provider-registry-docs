## Definition

A gender-of-entry value indicating that the establishment admits both boys and girls without restriction on grounds of sex.

## Scope Notes

- The recorded value reflects the school's admissions policy. Mixed schools remain bound by the Equality Act 2010 in respect of their treatment of pupils within the school.
