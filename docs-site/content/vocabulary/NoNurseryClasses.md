## Definition

A nursery-provision value indicating that the establishment does not operate any nursery classes.

## Scope Notes

- Applies where the nursery provision field is applicable to the establishment type (e.g. a primary school) but the school has no nursery classes. Distinct from the NotApplicable value, which is used where the field is irrelevant to the establishment type.
