## Definition

A special-class-provision value indicating that the establishment does not provide any specially resourced classes or units for pupils with special educational needs.

## Scope Notes

- Applies where the special-class provision field is applicable to the establishment type but no such provision is offered. Distinct from the NotApplicable value, which is used where the field is irrelevant to the establishment type.
