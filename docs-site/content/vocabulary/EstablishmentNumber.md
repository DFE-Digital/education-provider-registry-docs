## Definition

A one-to-four digit number assigned by the local authority to an establishment within its area. It is not globally unique - it is only meaningful together with the three-digit LA number. When combined with the LA number it forms the DfE number (written as "123/4567") and the LAESTAB (written without the slash as "1234567"), where the establishment number is zero-padded to four digits for display.
