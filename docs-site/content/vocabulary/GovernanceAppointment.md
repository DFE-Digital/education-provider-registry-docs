## Definition

A record of one named person holding one governance role at one establishment or group during a defined period. Identified by the GIAS Governance Identifier (GID). Carries the role type, appointment date, optional term-end date and appointing body.

## Scope Notes

- Maps to one GID row in the GIAS governance extract. Statutory basis for data collection: Education Act 1996 s.538. Policy purpose: national database of governors. Personal data - access controls required.
