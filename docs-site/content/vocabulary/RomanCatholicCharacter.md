## Definition

A religious-character value indicating that the school has been designated by the Secretary of State as having Roman Catholic character, conferring status as a Catholic school.

## Scope Notes

- Religious character designation is conferred under s.69(3) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. Roman Catholic schools may be voluntary aided or academy schools. The designating body is the relevant Catholic diocese.
