## Definition

An eight-digit identifier issued by the UK Register of Learning Providers (UKRLP), operated by HESA on behalf of the education and training sector. A UKPRN is required by institutions receiving public funding and provides a cross-sector link between GIAS and further- and higher-education data systems.
