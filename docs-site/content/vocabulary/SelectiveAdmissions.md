## Definition

An admissions-policy value indicating that the school selects all or some pupils by reference to academic ability or aptitude.

## Scope Notes

- Grammar schools selecting wholly by ability are permitted under s.104 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. Partial selection by aptitude in certain subjects is permitted for some maintained schools under SSFA 1998. Selective admissions must comply with the School Admissions Code.
