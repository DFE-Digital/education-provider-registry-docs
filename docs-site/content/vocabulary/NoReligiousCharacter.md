## Definition

A religious-character value indicating that the school has no religious character designation; it is a non-faith school.

## Scope Notes

- Applies to maintained and independent schools for which a religious character designation under the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 is applicable but has not been conferred. Distinct from NotApplicableReligiousCharacter, which is used where the designation concept does not apply to the establishment type at all.
