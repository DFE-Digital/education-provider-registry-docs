## Definition

An academy established through the voluntary conversion route, where an existing school or provider chose to convert to academy status under the Academies Act 2010.

## Scope Notes

- Converter academies retain their existing character but leave local-authority maintenance on conversion. Applies to mainstream academies, academy special schools, academy alternative provision and academy 16 to 19 establishments.
