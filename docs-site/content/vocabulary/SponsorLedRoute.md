## Definition

An academy established through the sponsor-led route, where an approved sponsor was appointed to govern new or replacement provision under the Academies Act 2010.

## Scope Notes

- Sponsor-led academies are typically replacements for underperforming schools or newly created provision. Applies to mainstream academies, academy special schools, academy alternative provision and academy 16 to 19 establishments.
