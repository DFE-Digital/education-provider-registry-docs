## Definition

A classification value indicating that a special-class provision classification does not apply to this type of establishment.

## Scope Notes

- Used for establishment types where the concept of resourced SEN classes is inapplicable, such as special schools (where all provision is by definition specialist) or early years settings. Distinct from NoSpecialClasses, which indicates the field is applicable but no such provision exists.
