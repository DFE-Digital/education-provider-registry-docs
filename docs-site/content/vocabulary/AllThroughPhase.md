## Definition

An education phase covering the full compulsory school age range within a single establishment, spanning both primary and secondary years (typically ages 4 or 5 to 16 or 18).

## Scope Notes

- All-through schools are not separately defined in statute but are recognised in DfE guidance. The ministerial phase determination under the Education Act 1996 governs the applicable accountability and funding regime.
