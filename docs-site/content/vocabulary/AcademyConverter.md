## Definition

A maintained school or other eligible institution that converted to academy status under the Academies Act 2010, entering into a funding agreement with the Secretary of State.

## Scope Notes

- This is an establishment type, not a trust type. The converting school retains its existing character but leaves local-authority maintenance on conversion.
