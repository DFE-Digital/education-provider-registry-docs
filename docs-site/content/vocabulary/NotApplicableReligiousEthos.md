## Definition

A classification value indicating that the religious ethos field does not apply to this type of establishment.

## Scope Notes

- Used for establishment types where recording a religious ethos is not applicable, such as further education colleges or local authority services. Distinct from NoReligiousEthos, which indicates the field is applicable but the school has declared no religious ethos.
