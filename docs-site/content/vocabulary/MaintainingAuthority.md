## Definition

A local authority acting in its specific statutory role as the body responsible for maintaining a maintained school under the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (SSFA 1998), s.21. The maintaining authority funds the school, employs staff in community schools, and is accountable for standards.

## Scope Notes

- Distinct from the LocalAuthority concept: a local authority may be recorded against any establishment for geographic or administrative purposes, but MaintainingAuthority applies only where the LA bears statutory responsibility for the school under SSFA 1998. Academies, independent schools and FE colleges do not have a maintaining authority.
