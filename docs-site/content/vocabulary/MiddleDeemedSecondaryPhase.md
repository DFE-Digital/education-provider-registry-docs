## Definition

An education phase assigned to a middle school where the Secretary of State has determined it should be treated as a secondary school, typically because the majority of its age range falls within secondary years.

## Scope Notes

- The deemed primary or secondary classification is a ministerial determination under the Education Act 1996 s.8 and associated regulations. It affects inspection framework, funding, and teacher pay conditions. Middle schools typically serve ages 8-12 or 9-13.
